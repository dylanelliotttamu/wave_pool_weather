What's "normal" for this pool on a given date is based on a multi-year historical baseline — not a forecast. Pool water temperature is reconstructed with the site's WAVE-FEST thermal model driven by decades of historical weather; air temperature, wind, and sunshine come from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (ERA5 reanalysis); lightning potential is proxied from historical CAPE (convective available potential energy), since no free historical lightning-strike dataset exists.
