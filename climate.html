🌤️ Pool Climate Normals

What's "normal" for this pool on today's date, based on a multi-year historical baseline — not a forecast. Pool water temperature is reconstructed with the site's WAVE-FEST thermal model driven by decades of historical weather; air temperature, wind, and sunshine come from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (ERA5 reanalysis); lightning potential is proxied from historical CAPE (convective available potential energy), since no free historical lightning-strike dataset exists.

Climate data isn't available for this pool yet. It's generated by a monthly batch job (scripts/build_climatology.py) — check back after the next run.
