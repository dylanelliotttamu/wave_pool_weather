Why This Site Exists

I created Wave Pool Weather with the goal of providing accurate water temperature forecasts for wave pools around the world, beginning with Waco. The weather in Texas can change quickly, and figuring out what equipment to bring can be tricky — because pool water temperature usually does not match the air temperature.

This site automatically fetches real-time weather data, runs a physics-based thermal energy balance model, and delivers 7-day pool temperature forecasts so you always know which wetsuit to pack.
