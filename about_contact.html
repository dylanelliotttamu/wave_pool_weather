The story behind Wave Pool Weather

Why This Site Exists

I created Wave Pool Weather with one goal in mind: give surfers accurate water temperature forecasts for wave pools so they never show up under-dressed (or sweltering in a 5/4 on a warm Texas day). Weather in places like Waco can swing dramatically within a single week, and the pool water temperature rarely tracks the air temperature — it has its own thermal momentum driven by solar radiation, wind, and evaporative cooling.

No other resource was filling that gap. So I built one. The site automatically fetches real-time weather data every day, feeds it through a physics-based thermal energy balance model, and delivers 7-day pool temperature forecasts across multiple wave pool venues in the U.S.

Curious How the Forecasts Actually Work?

The temperature predictions aren't a guess — they're the output of a multi-component physics model driven by live NWS and Open-Meteo weather data. The Learn More page walks through every equation: solar absorption, longwave radiation, evaporative cooling, ground heat exchange, and the full wetsuit recommendation guide.
