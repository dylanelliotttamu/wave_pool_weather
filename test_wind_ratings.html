📋 Legend

Barrel wind: How offshore/favorable is wind for barreling waves
🟢 Epic - Wind is within ±15° of ideal offshore angle
🟡 Good - Wind is within ±45° of ideal offshore angle
🔴 Poor - Wind is unhelpful (not displayed)
Air wind: How favorable for launching airs
🟢 Epic - Peak launch direction for the break
🟡 Good - Near the peak launch direction (only shown if not Poor)
🔴 Poor - On-shore or unfavorable (not displayed)
Only positive ratings (Epic or Good) are displayed on forecast cards for a clean view of what's good today.
